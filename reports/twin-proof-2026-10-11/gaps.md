### Limits of this proof (measured, not claims)
- Scale: two real apps (Infisical, firecrawl) plus the kit's shop control, about 300 requests per run, on one developer machine. The p50/p99 and memory numbers are this machine's, not production's.
- Traffic: scripted from each app's own routes and e2e-test seeds, not real users' traffic. It exercises the changed paths on purpose; it is not a sample of production load.
- firecrawl's paid path: it ran against a stand-in for two Supabase reads (`standin.mjs`), because the changed lines (the credit check) cannot run at all without them. The stand-in returns one team at 10% credit usage; the real Supabase was never called.
- `kern-twin test`: it cannot yet replay a saved case of an app that needs `load: 'record'` (see the entry above).
